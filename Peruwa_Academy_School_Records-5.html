Your browser is blocking auto-save

This browser won't let the page save data automatically for a locally-opened file. The app will still work for this session — but before you close the tab, click "Save a backup file" below, and next time click "Load a backup file" to bring your data back.

Keep an offline backup too

Auto-save keeps everything in this browser, but that copy disappears if the browser data is ever cleared, the browser is uninstalled, or you switch computers. Download a backup file every so often as a second copy that isn't tied to this browser.

Exams / Terms

Each round of marks entry and report cards is tied to an exam sitting. Add a new one for every term — nothing already saved is ever touched.

Your records are safe across years and terms

Adding a new exam never deletes or overwrites previous ones. Every exam's marks are stored separately by student, subject, and exam — so 2026 Term 1, 2 and 3 stay exactly as entered when you add 2027 Term 1, and so on. Data is only ever removed if you press Delete on that specific exam below.

Start a new term / exam

Tip: use the same academic year for all three terms (e.g. 2026 Term 1, 2026 Term 2, 2026 Term 3), then move to 2027 when the new year starts.

Correction Policy

Only marks from 1 to 100 can be entered in each learning area, for both CAT and Exam scores. Anything outside that range is rejected. Edits are applied exactly as typed — nothing is changed automatically — and every change to an already-saved score is recorded below for accountability.
